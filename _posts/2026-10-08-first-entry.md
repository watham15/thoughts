# what is this?

I had been putting off on making a blog/technical writing site for myself (static, of sorts) where I would just write the content and not need to worry about hosting and deploying complex code every time i posted something new. 

On telegram, way back in 2021 I came across a beautiful site which only had text content. AND it was very clean. I asked the guy and after which i came to know about Static Site Generators and the specific one he was using - **Hugo**. Cut to 2025, I had tried deploying Hugo. Local first then deploying to github-pages. Which as expected did not work out smoothly. Lack of interest henceforth made me give up. 


Now we're in the big '26 and I decided to give the whole ordeal one more shot. This time I decided to try out *Jekyll*. Funny enough everything went smooth. Barebones site set up, theme loaded (this one. no frills minimal css). Hosted on github-pages, which, by the way has built-in support for Jekyll.

So going forward, expect yap.
